About me Research

James Thomas

I am currently a student at the University of Cambridge studying for a MASt in mathematics, also known as part III of the Mathematical Tripos, with a focus on theoretical physics.

My interests are generally clustered around general relativity; numerical and analytic explorations of black holes and gravitational waves, conformal techniques in relativity and asymptotically-flat spacetimes, differential geometry, and algebraic topology. They also include conformal field theories and quantum field theory in curved spacetimes.

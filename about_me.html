About me

I was born in the UK, but at around ten years old I moved to Nelson, New Zealand, where I would stay until university. I have always enjoyed thinking about mathematics, and I grew to love physics throughout high school. By the time I started university, I knew I wanted to focus on mathematical physics. To continue to pursue mathematical physics I have recently moved back to the UK, which has been a fun experience.

Outside mathematics and physics, I enjoy all types of video games; some of my favourites include Slay the Spire II, Terraria, Portal 2, and Void Stranger. I also like to go on walks; in 2024, I visited Japan and managed to climb Mt. Fuji.

Some photos from the summit.

I have studied Japanese since high school and continue to practise by reading and taking part in the local Japanese society. I recently passed the JLPT N2, which represents a great milestone for me!

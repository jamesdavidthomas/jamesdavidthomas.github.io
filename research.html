Research

During my undergraduate degree, I completed an honours thesis and several supervised research projects. Together with my supervisors, I am currently working on a paper related to what was achieved in my honours thesis.

Conformal Geodesics in Asymptotically Flat Vacuum Spacetimes

Honours thesis completed as part of my BSc(Hons) under the supervision of Dr. Chris Stevens and Dr. Mariem Magdy.

The thesis focuses on the conformal geometry in asymptotically flat vacuum spacetimes and the importance of conformal geodesics in defining the conformal Gauss gauge. This conformal Gauss gauge is, in particular, useful for numerical simulations of gravitational waves. Some properties of these conformal geodesics and their influence on the resulting gauge are discussed.

Energy and Momentum in General Relativity: Gravitational Waves in Asymptotically Flat Spacetimes

Summer research project completed under the supervision of Dr. Chris Stevens.

This project was about understanding the energy-momentum problem in general relativity. It presents the connection between various quantities defined in the two major approaches to understanding energy and momentum in asymptotically flat spacetimes: the Bondi-Sachs and Newman-Penrose formalisms.

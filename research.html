Research

During my undergraduate studies, I undertook some supervised research projects, and was also required to complete an Honours thesis.

Conformal Geodesics in Asymptotically Flat Vacuum Spacetimes

Honours thesis completed in line with degree requirements for the BSc(Hons) under the supervision of Dr. Chris Stevens and Dr. Mariem Magdy.

It focuses on the conformal geometry in asymptotically flat vacuum spacetimes and the importance of conformal geodesics in defining the conformal Gauss gauge. This conformal Gauss gauge is, in particular, useful for numerical simulations of gravitational waves. Some properties of these conformal geodesics and their influence on the resulting gauge are discussed.

Energy and Momentum in General Relativity: Gravitational Waves in Asymptotically Flat Space-Times

Summer research project completed under the supervision of Dr. Chris Stevens

The project was about understanding the energy-momentum problem in general relativity. It presents the connection between various quantities defined in the two major approaches to understanding energy and momentum in asymptotically flat spacetimes: the Bondi-Sachs and Newman-Penrose formalisms.
